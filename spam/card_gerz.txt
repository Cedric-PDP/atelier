### Beyond the card
These days, in the wild lands of northern Emeldarion, lives a shamanic people led by the sage Keria. This people cultivates plants and raises animals, all bound to and nourished by the element of fire. Many training courses from the Dragheïr military academy are held in this region. Their purpose is to teach an understanding of the element of fire. Gerz, a former commando, retired here. A friend of the military school's headmistress, he agreed to welcome the students onto his ranch so they could be trained.
