### Why it works
#### Start the engine
Verdant Fruit Cruncher, Thorn of Love, Golden Boar feed the composition with bonuses. The main axes are Earth. Each bonus point increases damage and healing in its element by 10% in the model.
#### Convert your bonuses
Verdant Fruit Cruncher + Protector of Ruins have the strongest individual offensive profiles in this composition. The deck reaches 2,800 useful simulated damage, with elimination estimated at 9 turns. Check the curve to see when the investment pays off.
#### Protect the composition
The model estimates 4,981 effective HP and 0 healing. Healing is limited: check whether the offensive tempo fits your plan.
