### Beyond the card
The history lessons at the Dragheïr military school often speak of Aidan. Child of the heir to the Fire runic weapon, he followed proudly in his father's footsteps. A master of his own martial arts, he was also fluent in the Dhöggeïr language. His abilities were recorded, and they now serve as the foundation for training their soldiers. Today, at his father's side, Aidan dwells in the Infernal City as a humanoid Abyssal. Lisa often speaks of her dreams, in which she becomes Aidan in his past everyday life — but also, at times, in his present suffering.
