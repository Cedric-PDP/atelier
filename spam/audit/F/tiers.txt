## SPAM tier list: frequently asked questions
What is the best SPAM card? In version 1.20, Moxilys, the Humming One tops the card tier list (tier S), ahead of Spirit of the Fauna and Lisa, the Dragonrider. The ranking combines attack, generation, defense, support, control and economy in one simulation model.
What is the best SPAM deck? Aleera + Sovereign of the Ether Falls (Aleera, Nightmare-Eater, Sovereign of the Ether Falls, Commando Gerz, Sovereign of Flames) is the best deck found by the engine for version 1.20, with an estimated elimination in 6 turns. Every deck in the tier list has its own page: composition, recommended talents, gameplan, Tox targets and opponent profiles.
How is the SPAM tier list computed? Every card and deck is simulated over 10 turns, with one active talent per card, against theoretical opponent profiles. Card tiers are relative to the catalogue (S = top 8%); deck tiers compare the score with the average random deck and the best deck found. It is not a win rate: the Methodology page details the assumptions.
How often is the tier list updated? With every new version of the official Spamdex: card data is checked daily and the tier list is recomputed automatically as soon as a change appears. Current version: 1.20, analysis of October 9, 2026.
