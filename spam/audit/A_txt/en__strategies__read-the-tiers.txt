# Beyond the letter S.
Use rankings to ask better questions about your decks.
3 min read Share
Illustration by Krackdur
01
## Strong card, coherent team.
A card’s tier summarizes several individual performances. It doesn’t measure compatibility with every team. A lower-tier card may provide exactly the generation your attacker needs. Partners and deck pages make that context visible.
02
## Two different scales.
Cards are ranked by percentile within the catalogue. Decks use a common relative index: 0% is the average random deck and 100% the best deck found. An accessible deck and an advanced-collection deck are therefore comparable on this scale. The percentage is never a win rate.
03
## A compass for experimentation.
Rankings come from a search with explicit assumptions and a diversity filter for the suggested compositions. They do not cover every matchup. Start with a list, adapt it to your collection, and save the variants you enjoy in the workshop.
