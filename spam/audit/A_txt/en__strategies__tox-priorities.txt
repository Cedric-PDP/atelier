# Make every Spamton count.
Understand free slots, replacements, and the potential of Tox.
4 min read Share
Illustration by Atto
01
## Look at the available slots.
Abilities and passives occupy a card’s three lines, and a Tox can take the place of either. Deck pages tell you what each line is worth, which to keep, which to sacrifice and where to put the Tox. An attractive Tox is only useful if you can place it on a compatible holder.
02
## Read the net gain.
When a Tox replaces a passive, the engine accounts for the value lost. A replacement proposal therefore represents an estimated net improvement to the deck’s score. The plan ranks options, but depends on the draws you actually get.
03
## Separate potential from budget.
Potential is simulated with Tox equipped from the start of combat; the plan follows the game instead: one purchase per shop opening, within the Spamtons the deck has generated, and only among the rarities the shop offers at that point — everything is common on turn 2, epics arrive around turn 6, legendaries much later. Draws remain random: prioritize the first accessible improvements rather than automatically pursuing the entire plan.
