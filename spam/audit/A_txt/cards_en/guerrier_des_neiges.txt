### Beyond the card
When the Fire King vanished, the peoples of Nordvinter heavily militarized to support Hadeïr. The Snow Warriors make the most of the extreme conditions — the continent's cold and the inner sea's heat — to build a fighting physique unmatched among the Eltherians. They are the pride of Dragheïr, and today's equivalent of the Light King's pack of old.
