### Beyond the card
Originally from the Lonely Isles, an anthropomorphic martial master carrying a magical artifact settled northwest of Emeldarion. With that artifact, Amphibian brought many inhabitants of the Water shaman village to life. In time, his most gifted students surpassed him. He therefore organized a succession tournament with one simple rule: “The winner receives the morphic staff.” The Apprentice Evoker, determined to transform his family into Anthropomorphs, prevailed and became the tribe’s new leader. He then ordered the construction of watering places suited to new animal species.
