### Beyond the card
Originally from the Lonely Isles, an anthropomorphic martial master carrying a metamorphosis artifact settled northwest of Emeldarion. Amphibian gave life to many of the village's inhabitants. This shamanic sanctuary then grew with Keria's help: in exchange for land, the reptilian used his staff to help Keria build up her fire tribe. Asked why he left the flying isles, he always answers that it's the story of a broken friendship — of diverging ideals he regrets more and more as time goes by.
