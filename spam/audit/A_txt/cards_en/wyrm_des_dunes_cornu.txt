### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Wyrms are a hostile fauna of the Shal'Zaar regions. Somewhere between a snake and a sandworm, these enormous creatures turn sand into crystal. The mines created by their activity are called Horned Dunes. Learning to avoid and to fight them is daily life in the Shal'Azd'Hun region. Crystals are a rare and precious resource for monster hunters — their purity allows any element to be inserted into them.
