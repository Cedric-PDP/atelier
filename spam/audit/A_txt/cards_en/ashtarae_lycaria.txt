### Beyond the card
When the Water Spirit began its regulatory mission, Lycaria noticed the overpopulation around the spirit's lake. In Nordvinter, while the Spirit held back the advance of the Crystal Lands, the Eternal took on a form combining Anthropomorph and Pixarch traits, with runes engraved on her body. Her aura recalls her closeness to the divine will. She makes sure no one abuses her leniency. She seeks to preserve the tranquility of the lake and its surroundings.
