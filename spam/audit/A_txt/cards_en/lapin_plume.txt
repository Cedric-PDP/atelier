### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Feathered Rabbits are among the easiest desert rodents to tame. They form a deep bond of trust with the desert tribes of Shal'Zaar. Able to sense danger like no other creature of the desert, and to fly, they are the ideal animal companion for a monster hunter. When a Feathered Rabbit panics, the Abyssals are never far away.
