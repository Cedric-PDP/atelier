### Beyond the card
Since the beginning of the pilgrimages, a cat similar to the imaginary panthers appears in the dreams of the Eltherians. It always indicates the location of the great sage of the sacred stone. The south of Lumethia is thus the starting point of every pilgrimage. At the start of these premonitory dreams, no one clearly understood the message. This feline appeared physically before those who ignored the call. It is in fact a request before it is a warning: the pilgrimage must be undertaken. The Olforgeurs, having heard the Eltherians speak of this cat, proposed to call it Catixy — a tribute to their Oniyx colleagues, gone since time immemorial.
