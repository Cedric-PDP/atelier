### Beyond the card
The world really needs a researcher, a true one — the greatest of all the Tokscyth and, objectively, the most handsome. The Artisan of Hours is an unrivalled inventor and a traveler who observes the world and records all his adventures in a single book. Originally from Lumethia, he built a superb flying machine that will let him reach the heart of the Lonely Isles. This dream of exploration allows him to perfect his already flawless knowledge.
