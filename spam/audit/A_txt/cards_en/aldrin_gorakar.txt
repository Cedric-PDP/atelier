### Beyond the card
They say six children were born with a rune on their hand. In Lumethia, not far from the sacred sage's stone, a noble family thirsting for adventure gave birth to Gorakar. This family long dreamed of adventure until the day their dream called them to make a pilgrimage. Summoned to the heart of the Lonely Isles, the young adventurer set off to join Aldrin. His parents, on a different journey, then called upon the most erudite of the Tokscyth. With his revolutionary flying machine, they will set off to meet a powerful Dhöggeïr at the heart of the flying islands.
