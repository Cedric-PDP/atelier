### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Green Guardian is a lizard deeply attached to its tree of life. When young, it leaves the nest to choose a shrub it will protect its whole life. It feeds on sap, plants and invasive insects. It helps the tree bud and spread its roots. The Green Guardian is native to Lumethia but was imported to Vaeloria and Emeldarion.
