### Beyond the card
Treasurer of Nyevielh, Vladislaus handled most of the documents for his regent. A renowned philosopher and researcher, he is the childhood friend of the Kings of Earth and Shadow. As nightmares made his lands uninhabitable, he was entrusted with fragments of the Codex. Vladislaus had a manor built with moving rooms — impossible to get around without following precise patterns. The duchess and some servants chose to follow him. The Codex has been heavily protected from nightmares for centuries. No one knows what became of them... Are they still alive?
