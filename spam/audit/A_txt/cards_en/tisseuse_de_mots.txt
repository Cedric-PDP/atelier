### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Weavers of Words inhabit the tropical jungle of Presolda, southeast of Emeldarion. They rank among the region’s greatest animal threats. These giant spiders imitate the cries of prey to lure in their predators. Once caught in the spiders’ webs, those predators become stores of food.
