### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the Fire Spirit spends whole days calming the fiery imbalance of Hadeïr. Valiant and full of energy, it roams the fire elemactive zones — zones it tirelessly puts out, no doubt to preserve the elemental balance. It has been glimpsed on rare occasions with the soldiers of Dragheïr. Witnesses speak of a canine form where no one would dare set foot.
