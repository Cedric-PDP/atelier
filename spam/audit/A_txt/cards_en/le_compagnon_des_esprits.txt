### Beyond the card
Around the lake of the Water Spirit, small rodents have made their home. The spirit companions play there and seem to communicate with butterflies. These curious little animals don't flee from Eltherians — quite the opposite. They've become a mascot of the northern mountainous region of Vaeloria. This peaceful ecosystem has found its balance far from any predator. These little furballs surely keep a secret deep in their burrows. But Lycaria makes sure no one disturbs the calm of the lake.
