### Beyond the card
These days, a shamanic people led by the sage Keria live in the wild lands of northern Emeldarion. They cultivate plants and raise animals, all attuned to and nourished by the element of Fire. Some of these animals received the blessing of the metamorphosis staff. Transformed into Anthropomorphs, the Wheat Mice now help the village function. These exceptional farmers guarantee the flawless quality of the Bleh and keep other animals and crows from damaging the crops.
