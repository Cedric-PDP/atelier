### Beyond the card
The Drako'vish ranges stretch across the north of Lumethia. They form a border with Vaeloria and Hadeïr, land of the Great Lions. As the lions clashed in the northern savanna, Sylvan died in one final battle. Following the sacrifice of this shared friend, the clan leaders heeded his last wish and accepted peace. They then took Leo in. Despite a growing interest in the Eltherians who resemble him, Leo considers the Great Lions his family. He works to have them seen as a species in their own right, like the Natus.
