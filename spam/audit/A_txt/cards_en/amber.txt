### Beyond the card
The great military fortress-city of Dragheïr is a dam between the ocean and the Blazing Sea, a place of exchange between Eltherians and Dhöggeïrs, and a city rich in symbols with a noble martial culture. Amber is one of those symbols: a female warrior who masters Water and Fire and represents the alliance between Hadeïr and Nordvinter. Legend says that one day, two heroes who oppose each other in every way will manage to free Hadeïr from the flames. They will purify Mount Vulcan and free the mother of the Dhöggeïr. Amber is a charismatic, combative figure.
