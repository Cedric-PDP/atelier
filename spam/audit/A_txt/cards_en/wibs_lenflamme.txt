### Beyond the card
Historical accounts tell us the Oniyx were not only inventors. Some used their intelligence to set off on adventure. The most famous was Lumisaros, incapable of staying put on a chair. In charge of developing Oniyxia's relations, he was part of an adventuring group known as the deviants. Wibs, nicknamed "l'enflammée", is one of them, born with a rare elemental affinity for her species. Adventuring at sea was one of her greatest dreams. She built ships to cross the ocean with Lumisaros's help. Those gigantic machines now sit in the port of Lumethia. The painting of the happy Oniyx hangs in every captain's cabin. Wibs is celebrated every year with a festival of laughter and songs all across the port.
