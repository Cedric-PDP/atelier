### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Polychrome Owl is an emblematic animal of southern Vaeloria. In the woods, its hunt can be recognized by a peculiar sound reminiscent of a casino roulette wheel. Its feathers, collected from the ground each morning, are used to stuff pillows said to bring good fortune. The presence of a Polychrome Owl in a nearby wood means that the forest is healthy, making the species highly valued by the Vaelorians.
