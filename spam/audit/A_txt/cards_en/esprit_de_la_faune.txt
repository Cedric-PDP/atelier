### Beyond the card
After the single continent shattered, the environment struggled to regain its balance because elemactive activity remained far too intense after the divine wars. The Eternal, wrongly nicknamed Spirit of the Fauna, took on a role of regulation. It sees to the needs of the living species on the Surface. Meeting it is exceedingly rare, unless you're its target. Be careful not to disturb it in its work. It knows how to punish poachers without the slightest mercy, through its powerful abilities.
