### Beyond the card
Godlur was the first artificial creature designed by Gabriel and Lumisaros. It protected the Pixarch capital from the most belligerent Eternals. This powerful golem could harness photosynthesis to recharge its integrated Oniyx orbs, allowing it to operate by day or night. Many described it as indestructible. Is it still active somewhere? Could it still be operating at the heart of the Lonely Isles?
