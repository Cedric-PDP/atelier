### Beyond the card
In the great forge of Logaïr, gigantic creatures churned the lava of Mount Vulcan. The unique work of the Minions of Flames — which no one could replicate at such temperatures — made the craftsmanship of Hadeïr prosperous and essential. Outside of Oniyxia, it was the Oniyx's favorite city. Many Olforgeurs worked the materials alongside passionate artisans. Without the Minions of Flames, the runic weapons could never have been forged. They made Logaïr the finest forge in the world in its time.
