### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. Incredible creatures inhabit the Lonely Isles, a cluster of flying islands above the Nimbus Sea. Among these species is the Celestial Dragosaur. Its nests can be observed on the cliffs of the continent of Aelther. Once adults, they head to the floating islands. No one knows why they go there, for now.
