### Beyond the card
Meridia is today a prosperous city. The council handles foreign diplomatic relations. It is the only country lucky enough to trade with the Alphilans. The princess, named after the sacred tree, Meridia, took a great interest in the sea folk. On the southern coasts, she met the wounded Lord of the Tides. She cared for him and there they cultivated their love. Their bond created a relationship between the two peoples. Today, the warrior Princess leads at the front alongside her husband. She is a new hope against the Abyssals of the Starry Sea.
