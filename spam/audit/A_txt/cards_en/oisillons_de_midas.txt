### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Hatchlings of Midas are said to be the offspring of the Glimmers of Hope. It hasn't yet been proven, due to the difficulty of Aelther's restrictions — its fauna and flora are hard to observe. Their nest is perched high, at the height of the tallest trees. They only become visible during the ascent of the Celestial Mount, when the summit comes to eye level.
