### Beyond the card
We have little information about Hadeïr beyond Dragheïr. We do know, however, that the Blazing Willow of Détwin Forest is an entity that must be protected. Able to cleanse creatures afflicted by corruption, it represents a source of hope for all of Hadeïr. Yet it cannot move and must be defended against the Abyssals. An Eltherian bastion was therefore established in the heart of the forest to keep them away from the saving entity. Dragons are the greatest known abyssal threat. In the region, some Dhöggeïrs attuned to Fire have become powerful corrupted Dragons. Repelling even one is nothing short of a miracle—and has already cost many soldiers their lives.
