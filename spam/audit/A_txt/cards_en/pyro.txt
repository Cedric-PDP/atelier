### Beyond the card
As the corruption spread within the walls of the city of Logaïr, Pyro, the greatest aviator of his time and loyal vassal of the Fire King, saw to the evacuation of the uninfected civilians. The history books tell of those final moments, which today make him one of the great saving figures within Dragheïr. As he began his painful abyssal transformation, he sealed the gates of the city of the forges before him. Cursed today alongside his King, Pyro, in the fearsome form of a phoenix, still dwells in the city now renamed the infernal city.
