### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. North of Emeldarion, you can see these otters come down from the Crystal Lands, passing through Abondance before swimming back up the waterway to start their mad descent all over again. You can even see them, sometimes, clinging to orbs they bring up from the seabed — a strange behavior the Alphilans keep secret.
