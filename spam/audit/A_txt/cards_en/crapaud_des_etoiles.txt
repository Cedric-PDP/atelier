### Beyond the card
After the single continent cracked apart, a gigantic inner sea formed, dotted with many islands. Some are held aloft by an unknown force above the Nimbus Sea. Others are true islands, made inaccessible by the presence of Abyssals. The Toad of the Stars is one of these monsters. With a single echoing croak, entire crews of sailors fall asleep. Those least fit to fight in the dream world are devoured. At the risk of becoming a fly at a giant toad's dinner, Nyevielh has been made inaccessible because of the danger of Toad of the Stars appearing.
