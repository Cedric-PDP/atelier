### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the shadow spirit spends whole days hunting the nightmares of Nyevielh. Its violent and dreadful activity has given it a most hostile appearance; the few Eltherians still alive prefer not to cross its path, for its prey is never far. A wooden statue was built in its honor at Port Rhyezeal, maintained there as a protective idol of Nyevielh and a scarecrow against abominations.
