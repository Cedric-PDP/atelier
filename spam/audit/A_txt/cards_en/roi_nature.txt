### Beyond the card
Being the King of an element is an honorary title. It signifies a high responsibility in the elemental balance. The Kings had their heirs equipped with a runic weapon. However, the only King without an heir is the King of Nature. This Pixarch, his wings mysteriously torn off, bound himself to the weapon. In keeping with the texts of the Primordial Codex according to Faerylia, these weapons would make it possible to seal the Deities and win freedom. He reigned over Vaeloria for centuries. When the disease of Fanure appeared, he gave his life creating the Meridia.
