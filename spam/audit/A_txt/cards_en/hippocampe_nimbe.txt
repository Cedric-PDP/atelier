### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The clear waters of the Nimbus Sea stretch along the southern shores of the new Vaeloria. The Nimbus Seahorse is the emblematic animal of this vast expanse. Males carry the fertilized eggs, while females protect both the males and their broods with their dorsal fins. The Alphilans use these creatures to signal their presence at the Surface. Ignoring that warning may provoke a dangerous and needless conflict.
