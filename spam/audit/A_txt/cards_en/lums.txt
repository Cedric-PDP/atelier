### Beyond the card
Lums is a renowned singer from Abondance. Her performances draw on her two personalities and her fascination with the Starry Sea; the stars and the ocean are her favorite themes. She began her career at the Katrobu bar, which also welcomed the Mistress of Tints. The two artists care for one another like sisters despite their differences. The singer’s next tour, “Thalassophobia,” is a sound-and-light show. On several songs, she will be accompanied by artwork from the Mistress of Tints. The tour appears to herald a major turning point in one of their careers.
