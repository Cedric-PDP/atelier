### Beyond the card
The Natus are a new tribal species made up of few individuals. They are very attached to their territory and seem to cultivate their own beliefs. Their superior intellect sets them apart from mere animals. In the Monts Nomades of Nordvinter, or in the scorching desert of Shal'Zaar, one can find very different ones and their villages. The Hibernatus of Nordvinter sometimes welcome freezing adventurers in exchange for stories.
