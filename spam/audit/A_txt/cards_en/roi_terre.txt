### Beyond the card
The title of elemental King is honorary, but carries immense responsibility for maintaining elemental balance. The Kings equipped their heirs with runic weapons. According to Faerylia’s interpretation of the Primordial Codex, those weapons would allow them to seal the Deities and win their freedom. The Earth King was a herald and spokesman for the Eternals, Phahl'Aon of Shal'Zaar — the richest and most advanced expanse of the old days. When the kingdom fell, he vanished without a trace.
