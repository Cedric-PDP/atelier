### Beyond the card
As Vaeloria struggled to recover from the disappearance of the Nature King, the Sengo bandit clan terrorized the northwest. Ignea lived peacefully with her nomadic family until the day she hid from danger and watched the bandits slaughter everyone she loved. She then devoted her life to vengeance, training, and battle. While hunting the clan’s leader, she freed several villages and finally earned the duel to the death she had long sought. Despite her opponent’s countless underhanded schemes, she prevailed, took command of the clan, and transformed it into Vaeloria’s present-day adventurers’ guild.
