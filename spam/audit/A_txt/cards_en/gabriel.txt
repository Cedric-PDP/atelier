### Beyond the card
History books recount this legend in many forms: the story of Aurelia, the Eternal who gave birth to the Pixarchs, and of her first creation, Gabriel. Both bound their fate to that of their people. Gabriel was a brilliant inventor and the closest friend of the Oniyx Lumisaros. Together, they created innovations that transformed everyday life, as well as legendary artifacts. The loss of the great winged beings during Aurelia’s Dawn is still mourned, although some survived the event, including the Nature King and Keria.
