### Beyond the card
Eternals devoted to the preservation of the Surface appeared after certain catastrophes. Their purpose is to keep the mistakes that were made from happening again. Protector of the ruins of a gigantic worksite of the past, the one they call the Eye of the Mountains can be observed not far from the Ether Falls, southwest of Emeldarion, or northwest of Shal'Zaar. Some pilgrims who have glimpsed it see a good omen in it. Others who have faced it are no longer here to tell of it.
