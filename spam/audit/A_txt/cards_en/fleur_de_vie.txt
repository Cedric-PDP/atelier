### Beyond the card
After the Light King abandoned Aelther, flowers with healing potential began to grow on the Celestial Mount. Some pilgrims pick them despite the bans and fail to complete their pilgrimage — but it lets them heal a loved one or earn a living in Abondance. The greatest of the Flowers of Life sits atop the highest mountain. It is made of gigantic petals and never opens. Everyone wonders what could be inside. Will a chosen one one day be able to open it? Is there a miracle remedy inside?
