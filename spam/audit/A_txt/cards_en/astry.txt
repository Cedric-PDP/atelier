### Beyond the card
Spatiotemporal displacements are rarely witnessed. Young Astry creates rifts similar to Vivy's. He appears alone on the beaches of Aelther, far from the pilgrim paths. We were lucky enough to hear him say his own name while talking to himself. He seems to stay around the western coasts, where he fights abyssal monsters emerging from the Starry Sea. His supernatural powers and spectacular battles leave onlookers speechless. His apparent youth and his powers raise questions about his origin. No one dares disturb him, following the directives imposed on the continent of Aelther.
