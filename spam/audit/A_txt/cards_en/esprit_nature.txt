### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the nature spirit spends whole days fighting against Fanure. Today, it tries to protect the Meridia from withering. This great tree of life is the symbol of Vaeloria. The spirit toils in the unfathomable depths of its roots. Its barks can sometimes be heard in this chaotic labyrinth. The few adventurers who brave the Roots are regularly exposed to elemactive zones. These seem to be erased by the passing of the canine, marking out a new path.
