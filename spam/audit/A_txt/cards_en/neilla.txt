### Beyond the card
Among the Eternals, Spirits, and other mythical beings is Neilla. This singular entity creates currents and keeps the waters from overflowing. Those who follow the currents may glimpse the demigoddess for the briefest instant. Countless sailors, captivated by her beauty and her secrets, have set out in search of the beautiful Alphilan. Yet the most dangerous routes spare not even the boldest travelers. After so many ships and lives were lost, a treaty was enacted forbidding the use of vessels to hunt the Lady.
