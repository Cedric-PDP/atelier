### Beyond the card
They say six children were born with a rune on their hand. In Shal'Zaar, in a village of monster hunters, a young girl can communicate with her mind. The moment she forms a link, her eyes seem filled with a purplish liquid. With her power, she tamed some of the most terrifying creatures of the Presolda jungle — tropical snakes and other dinosaurs that now aid monster hunters. For all these feats, she earned the title of Heraldess, a divine envoy echoing the vanished regent.
