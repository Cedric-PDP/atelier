### Beyond the card
The Drako’vish ranges stretch across northern Lumethia, forming a border with Vaeloria, land of the Great Lions. Faced with a powerful wave of Fire corruption, the King of the Blazing Flames and his clan were forced to seek new lands. He had no choice but to move his subjects onto Aurora’s territory. His body, corrupted by Fire, took on the appearance of an Eltherian. Aurora denied him any land, igniting a violent war. The two clans fought for days in the savanna of northern Lumethia. The conflict ended with the tragic death of Sylvan, a friend to both sides.
