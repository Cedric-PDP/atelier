### Beyond the card
The corruption of Hadeïr by Fire is characterized by its Poison Gas. This powerful elemactive phenomenon drifts with the wind and causes countless afflictions. The deadly haze originates at the heart of Mount Vulcan, where the Kings’ runic weapons were forged. Deposits recovered from the bottom of several bodies of water suggest an underground source.
