### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Floral Giraffe is the pilgrims' favorite mount. They can grow crops on their Giraffe's coat within a few days. In Vaeloria, this noble animal provides a mobile vegetable garden available in all circumstances — any family worthy of the name owns its own Floral Giraffe.
