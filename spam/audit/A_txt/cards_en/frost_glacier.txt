### Beyond the card
They say six children were born with a rune on their hand. In Nordvinter, within the great warrior tribe of the Nords, the chief's son Frost was born with strange animal features. Despite an ability to resist the cold, even the most intense, he endured much harassment. His parents' death gave him no more authority. It was only once his pilgrimage was complete that he was respected. Today, he is the master of the Dhöggeïr prince: Glacier. He is also the vice-president of the council of the Dragheïr military school. There he is recognized for his exploits.
