### Beyond the card
In the region of Azeel, whose woods stretch northwest of Vaeloria, one can find non-aggressive creatures whose simple goal is to scare. The Bouha thus becomes a tourist spot for its tests of courage. The trial there is watched over by the Nocturnal Watcher. If a visitor panics dangerously, this powerful owl appears. It brings people back to a safe zone. It also speaks the common tongue very well. Its magical power carries a heavy secret that it will never reveal.
