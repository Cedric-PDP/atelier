### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Imaginary Panther teleports through spatial rifts similar to Vivy's. These rifts are traced toward the arrival point by one of the eyes present on its body. It is sometimes found on the beaches of Vaeloria, where it lets itself be observed. It seems to commute there from the Lonely Isles. The panther is neutral, even though its appearance suggests an elemental corruption.
