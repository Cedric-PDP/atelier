### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the environmental imbalance couldn't easily right itself — because of far too much elemactive activity in the aftermath of the divine war. The Eternal Verdelune took on a role of regulation. It sees to the needs of the growing plants on the Surface. Meeting it is exceedingly rare and heralds good harvests. Be careful not to disturb it in its work — its disappearance, they say, would wither the crops and pollute the waters.
