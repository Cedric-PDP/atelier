### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Verdant Fruit Cruncher is a herbivore from Vaeloria that loves barely-ripe fruit fallen to the ground. Usually pretty inactive, its belly lets it stock up reserves for times when fruit is scarce — though sometimes they're found raiding food storehouses in the cold season.
