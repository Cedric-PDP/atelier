### Beyond the card
These days, in the wild lands of northern Emeldarion, lives a shamanic people led by the sage Keria. This people cultivates plants and raises animals, all bound to and nourished by the element of fire. Many training courses from the Dragheïr military academy are held in this region. The Pyrokinetic Sorceress, headmistress of this prestigious school, sought a way to train her students against the fire corruption. She then reached an agreement with Keria.
