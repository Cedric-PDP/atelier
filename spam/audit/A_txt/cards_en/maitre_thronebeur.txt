### Beyond the card
The history books tell of a society in which each artisan specialized in a single trade, built on collective efficiency and trust in one's neighbor. The Olforgeurs are stocky beings, barely shorter than Eltherians and very broad-shouldered. They have always been associated with the Oniyx through a shared passion for craft. Master Blacksmith Thronebéur, is an Olforgeur legend. His story keeps alive, over a good drink, the memory of comrades fallen in battle. It also celebrates knowledge passed down through generations. He is said to have been a close friend of the great Oniyx chief.
