### Beyond the card
In the Crystal Lands east of Nordvinter, humanoids accompanied by wolves have been spotted on multiple occasions. These travelers of the icy peaks seem to be scouting the frozen zone. They have already attacked several worksites aiming to free the Oniyx technologies from the ice. Since then, no one dares try again, on pain of reprisals. They seem peaceful toward the youths of Nordvinter searching for their Dhöggeïr. Nothing is known of their origin. Their esoteric equipment suggests they come from Emeldarion.
