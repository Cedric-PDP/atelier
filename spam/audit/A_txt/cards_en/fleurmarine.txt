### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. Along the western coasts of Lumethia, one can see clusters of algae coming from the Ocean of the Depths. These beaches are off-limits because of very aggressive Marineflowers that may have settled there. These sea eels are quite feared by fishermen. However, they are a delicacy, a specialty of the ports of Lumethia.
