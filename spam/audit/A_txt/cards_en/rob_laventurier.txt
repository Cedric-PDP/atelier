### Beyond the card
Not all Tokscyth are just alchemists. Some care more about the thrill of adventure. They contribute a great deal to their kin's research: their knowledge of plants, ores and mushrooms helped create TOX — a substance with random effects that are not always without consequences. The adventurers of this species are small, fearless daredevils from Hadeïr, braving mountains and seas in search of the very best for their creations.
