### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. Thorn of Love is a hedgehog whose spines are said to be hallucinogenic — widely used in TOX research to reduce side effects. Few of these echidnas are still found in Vaeloria, but pilgrims report running into them along the way in Emeldarion and Aelther. Talks are underway to reintroduce the species to its natural habitat.
