### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The masked Foxes, faithful to the King of Shadow, have settled in the mountains east of Nyevielh. These trained creatures wear a mask as a sign of loyalty. They face the abyssals prowling around their territory. This region is too cold for many Eltherians to live there. But the canine guardians of the Gleam of the White Fox ensure their safety.
