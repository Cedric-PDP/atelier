### Beyond the card
At the heart of Mount Vulcan lived a powerful Dhöggeïr. Its nest became the foundation of Logaïr’s first structures, and the great Forge City gradually grew under its protection. A bond of trust developed between the creature and the successive Fire Kings. Each King taught the Dhöggeïr language to his heir so that the new ruler could communicate with it in turn. No one knows what became of the creature after the corruption of Fire, but Hadeïr’s condition bodes ill. Many believe it took the form of an Abyssal Dragon.
