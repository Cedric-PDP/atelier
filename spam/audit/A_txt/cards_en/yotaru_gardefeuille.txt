### Beyond the card
Sir Leafguard is a Nymph in the service of the Dhöggeïr Emeraldoth. Since the latter isn't the most eloquent, Yotaru served as his messenger and interpreter. Brave and warm-hearted, he sacrificed much of himself to meet the needs of his lizard friend. Today, in the Marais'Cage south of Emeldarion, he makes sure pilgrims respect the area's rules. No one may leave the road or set foot on the ground. Guided by such a living legend, the pilgrims gladly comply.
