### Beyond the card
Elther would not exist without the Deities; all life is the product of divine creation. The creators each have their own works and doctrines. The runic language used by these supreme beings is an incomprehensible score. Only the appearance of certain runes remains accessible to the inhabitants of the Surface. The Melody of Divinities is a religious chant that has crossed the ages. The work gives thanks to the divine. The chant's incomprehensible psalms are studied to better understand the text.
