### Beyond the card
They say six children were born with a rune on their hand. In Dragheïr, the slums see many war orphans struggle to survive. This atrocious military meritocracy, beginning at the earliest age, gave rise to Lisa. As a child, she showed unmatched combat abilities as well as a mastery of the Dhöggeïr language. A noble family, that of Pyra, decided to adopt her for these feats. Her studies are funded, and she becomes president of the military academy's council.
