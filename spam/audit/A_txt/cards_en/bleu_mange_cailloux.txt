### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Blue-Stone-Eater is a small dinosaur from the jungles of Presolda. Its eggs are incubated by the heat of the caves in southern Emeldarion. They survive alone or with their siblings and feed on ores and rocks of all kinds. Their adult form depends on their diet. Among those recorded are the Celestial Dragosaurs of Aelther and of the Dunes of Shal'Zaar. The latter are tamed as mounts by monster hunters.
