### Beyond the card
The woods of Azeel stretch northwest of Vaeloria and are home to non-aggressive creatures whose sole purpose seems to be frightening visitors. Bouha has therefore become a tourist destination famed for its tests of courage. These trials are staged by the Forest Accomplices, the most imposing of whom is the Alphilan Moxilys. Each visitor follows a personalized route through the woods. No one knows why the service is free, a secret concealed behind wordless humming. Moxilys is an emblematic figure in the village of Alween and accompanies every brave challenger with a song.
