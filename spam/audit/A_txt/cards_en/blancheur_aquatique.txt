Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The coral reefs between Lumethia and Vaeloria, where Alphilan communities have settled, are home to axolotls with a passion for music and painting. Many Aquatic Whitenesses cross the Nimbus Sea to reach Abondance, where they appear to relish the city’s rich blend of cultures.
