### Beyond the card
The Pixarch disappearance left the Anthropomorphs with a heavy legacy. The Astral Healer is proof of the survival of the protectors of the Tree of Mysteries. She can be encountered in the forest of Vaelor. She is searching for "Gabriel's staff", an artifact capable of metamorphosis, stolen after an internal conflict that cost the Samouraï Croc his life. As she was away during the Fanure epidemic, she had entrusted the staff to her second, who lost it in single combat. All she knows about the culprit is that he is probably somewhere in Vaeloria.
