### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the light spirit spends whole days guiding pilgrims to the Celestial Mount of Aelther. Its social nature with the pilgrims makes it the perfect mascot. Before the great ascent, on the paths and in the villages of Okaemi, the pilgrims are always delighted to look after it. It can sometimes be seen intimidating those who break the rules of the continent.
