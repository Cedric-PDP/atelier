### Beyond the card
The Drako'vish ranges stretch across the north of Lumethia. They form a border with Hadeïr, land of the Great Lions. This region suffered heavy corruption by Fire. The creatures touched and infected often have only one hope: the Blazing Willow of the Détwin woods. Catimimi was among the young infected lions. Barely saved by the Willow, he often tells the story of his king leading him to the woods. Catimimi witnessed the end of the battle between his kin. Today, he is tasked with protecting Leo.
