### Beyond the card
The Drako’vish ranges stretch across northern Lumethia, forming a border with Vaeloria, land of the Great Lions. During a journey devoted to his research, Sylvan took in an abandoned child named Leo. When he learned that a bloody war had broken out between his childhood friends, Catimimi urged him to intervene and end the fighting. Sylvan brought about the long-awaited truce, but suffered mortal wounds in the process. With his final words, he asked that Leo be cared for. His sacrifice ended the war.
