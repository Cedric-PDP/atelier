### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. Pods of unusual cetaceans migrate from the Ocean of the Depths. Prankster Whales delight in teasing Lumethian ships, often greeting them by pretending to capsize them. Their journeys are equally strange: they travel back and forth between the Ocean of the Depths and the Nimbus Sea. The Alphilan people are clearly involved somehow, but the mystery remains unsolved.
