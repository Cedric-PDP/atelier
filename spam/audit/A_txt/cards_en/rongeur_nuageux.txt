### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. Cloudy Rodents live on the clouds of the Celestial Mount, which they devour like cotton candy. Native to the Eternals’ Celestial Realms, where they are considered an invasive species, they sometimes cross the sky aboard cumulonimbus clouds that have drifted away from their realm.
