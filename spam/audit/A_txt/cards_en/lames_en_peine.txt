### Beyond the card
Death does not exist in Elther. In the Abyss, the dreams and nightmares of the Awakened mingle to purge damned souls: dreams shape new souls, which nightmares then purify. Wander Blades is a painting depicting a confrontation with the Calamity of Shadow. In it, the Nightmare Reaper stands against the creature in Azeel’s Cemetery of the Void. Displayed in churches throughout the world, the work exalts duty and self-sacrifice in service of the common good.
