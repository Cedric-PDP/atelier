### Beyond the card
Elther would not exist without the Deities: all living things are the product of divine creation. Aeltheria is the creator of every Deity. Her favored form is said to be that of a she-wolf, making the animal a sacred and deeply revered figure throughout Elther. On the day of the Great Cataclysm, the Awakened peoples joined forces to seal away the Deities; the creature they imprisoned had the form of a gigantic she-wolf. Many theories exist, but all draw a connection to Aeltheria. Faerylia is being actively sought, as she may hold answers on the matter.
