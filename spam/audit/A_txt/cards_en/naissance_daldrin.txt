### Beyond the card
Since time immemorial, the Dhöggeïr are born from great eggs high up in the mountains. These legendary creatures are the arbiters of the battles between Eternals, protecting the Surface. Aldrin and Emeraldoth are the firstborn. They have often disagreed, though brothers from the same egg. As the single continent found itself fragmented, the two protected the living with the full extent of their powers. Southern Vaeloria was renamed Emeldarion in their honor. Aldrin is said to have found rest on the Lonely Isles, and his brother in the Marais'Cage of Emeldarion.
