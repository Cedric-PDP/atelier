### Beyond the card
Faerylia was renowned as the most learned and cultivated person in Shal’Zaar. A vassal of the Earth King, she mastered magic and the written word like no one else. Alone, she succeeded in deciphering the Primordial Codex. Time revealed, however, that she had disclosed only a fraction of the grimoire’s true contents. She explained precisely how to seal the Deities, from forging the runic weapons to constructing the portal. After attempting to claim powers akin to those of the Deities, she was punished with the destruction of her homeland. The Weaver of Chaos is now the world’s most wanted witch.
