### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Golden Boar is part of the wildlife living in the forests of Aelther. It's mostly found at the foot of the Celestial Mount, where it feeds on gold and roots. This diet strengthens its gold-rich skeleton and makes it quite dangerous to provoke. Pilgrims have a rule to feed only on Honey Caterpillars there. That way, the continent's species are protected from poaching.
