### Beyond the card
According to many historical accounts, the war between the higher magical species and the Eternals was raging. Armed with their finest weaponry, blending elemental magic and weapons of mass destruction, the Oniyx bought the time needed to seal the Deities. The most fearsome machine, Paguroniyx, was piloted by the Inventor. No one knows where this machine is today. Perhaps trapped in the ice of the crystal lands? Or maybe it sank into the ocean depths?
