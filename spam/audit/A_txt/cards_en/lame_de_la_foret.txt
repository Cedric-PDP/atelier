### Beyond the card
The Blades of the Forest were a people of Nymphs who protected the Pixarch woods. Often tasked with guarding a single tree or a forest clearing, they delighted in life’s simple pleasures and loved to play pranks. They were also fearsome warriors. No one knows whether their people still exist today. The Blades are believed to dwell within the Lonely Isles. Further research may reveal whether any members of their species survived.
