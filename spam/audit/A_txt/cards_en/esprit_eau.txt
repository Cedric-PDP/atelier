### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the Water Spirit spends whole days slowing the advance of the ice of the Crystal Lands. Active in a huge lake of Nordvinter, it is the only clearly visible spirit accessible to a large population. This peculiarity never fails to attract many travelers. However, disturbing a spirit's activity has a price that Lycaria, Shadow of the White, is ready to make one pay, so that no one bothers the little aquatic canine.
