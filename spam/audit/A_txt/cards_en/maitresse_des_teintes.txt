### Beyond the card
They say that six children were born with a rune on their hand. In Abondance, in Katrobu’s pleasure district, the Nameless struggle to build a future for themselves. A rebellious young artist repaints entire sections of the city every day. Constantly pursued by the authorities, she nevertheless drew countless tourists with her work. The Mistress of Tints thus became a celebrity with followers throughout the world. She is said to have visions of her nightmares without ever experiencing ordinary dreams. She paints them to release them and preserve her mind.
