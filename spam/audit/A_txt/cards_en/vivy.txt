### Beyond the card
East of Emeldarion lives a very territorial martial tribe. The little we know of them are strong wolf motifs. Lightning seems to strike their region constantly. Researchers believe the Light King and his descendants may have settled there. The theory has never been confirmed. A spatial rift always opens before anyone who crosses the line of the canine totems. Vivy always appears first. Followed by her Shinobi, she threatens to attack anyone who would go further into the lands of the Pack.
