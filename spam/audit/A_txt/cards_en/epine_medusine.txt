### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. On the northern coasts of Hadeïr, you can see flaming jellyfish flying all the way to the center of the Blazing Sea. Their hot body allows their ascent before falling slowly back into the water. Underwater, they store up heat to begin their weekly cycle again. The Ignited Lizards, imported from Emeldarion, and the Medusine Thorns create a grand nighttime spectacle that is a source of national pride and the theme of many festivals.
