### Beyond the card
Long ago, Gabriel and Lumisaros built a mysterious tree within the Pixarch capital. The tree could reclaim surplus elemactive energy to cleanse the environment. Today, the ruins are fragmented into a flying archipelago above the Nimbus Sea. These Lonely Isles are a dream expedition for researchers. The tree is theorized to be responsible for the appearance of Floral Zombies — humanoid in form and in a vegetative state. They fall a few at a time from the heights of the islands and are washed onto the coasts of Vaeloria. They are weak abyssal monsters and an excellent subject of study.
