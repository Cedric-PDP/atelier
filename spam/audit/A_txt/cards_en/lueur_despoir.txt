### Beyond the card
Now that the world seems to have regained its elemental balance, nature and its adaptation to the environment can be observed as never before. The Glimmers of Hope are the only neutral creatures in Nyevielh’s forest. They are said to mark the path to Vladislaus’s manor, and their presence also means that no abyssal monster is nearby. Do not stop to question this forest: if any protocol has been left for the unfortunate souls who dare enter, follow its instructions.
