### Beyond the card
With the single continent freshly shattered, the earth spirit spends its days fighting in the corrupted desert of Shal'Zaar. Always on the move, it keeps the mines stocked with materials and battles the Abyssals. You can still see the marks of some of its long fights. A few monster hunters were lucky enough to catch it in action, and love to tell of their battle alongside the brave sand wolf.
