### Beyond the card
Many accounts mention the peoples of old, including an ancestral species of small humanoids with feline features. Now extinct, we keep their courage and sacrifice in memory. In the fight against the Eternals, all the Oniyx communities created an entity of controlled chaos: the Prisoner of the Machine. Struck by such a weapon, an Eternal is sealed within the sphere. Made essentially of magic, a prisoner is kept in a state of deep sleep. Its regenerative capacity becomes an inexhaustible source of energy — still powering the autonomy of the Oniyx factories, trapped in the ice of the Crystal Lands.
