### Beyond the card
Abondance, the economic capital of Emeldarion, is as rich in natural resources as it is in culture. The full diversity of its population can be seen there. Yet choosing to live in this divided city is no easy matter. Often poorly informed, residents find themselves in need of help. Several companies control the area, and the most prosperous hold seats on the city’s ruling council. Clans such as the Wandering Feline form in the hope of gaining influence. This one welcomes Anthropomorphs, gives them guidance, and provides them with housing.
